iTurut – Smart Voice Note-Taking App

Minimal Touch. Maximum Voice.

🚀 What is iTurut?

iTurut is a bilingual, voice-first note-taking app that captures thoughts in real time -- while driving, working, or walking.

🧑‍🏫 Why I Built iTurut

As an educator, digital marketer, database admin and poet, I needed a tool that let me capture spontaneous ideas -- especially in Turkish.

🧠 The Problem

  • Short, clunky recordings
  • English-only limitations
  • No smart command handling
  • No intelligent editing

🤖 The Solution

  • One-tap to start
  • 3-minute uninterrupted sessions
  • Voice commands: "Stop", "New note", "Edit", "Send"
  • GPT-powered editing
  • Multilingual support (TR + EN)

💸 Business Model

  • Free: 2 notes/day
  • 7-day trial for full features
  • $20 one-time unlock
  • $2/month for AI extras

📈 Roadmap & Vision

  • MVP live by Q3 2025
  • Future: web dashboard, in-car voice UI, smart tags

👤 The Founder

Istikbal Turut – educator, poet, database admin, digital thinker. Building tools that matter in both Turkish and global voice ecosystems.
