Privacy Policy for iTurut App

Last updated: June 16, 2025

iTurut is a voice-based note-taking application designed to help users quickly record and save their ideas. Your privacy is very important to us. This policy outlines how we handle your information.

1. What We Collect

  • Voice input: Processed through Microsoft Azure Speech-to-Text to convert your speech into written notes.
  • User information: Your email is collected only when signing in for cloud features.
  • Note content: Notes are stored securely in Firebase Firestore and accessible only by you.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To convert your voice into text notes
  • To store notes in the cloud for device syncing
  • To authenticate your identity and personalize access

3. Do We Share Your Data?

No. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Data Security

We use secure technologies like Firebase Auth and Firestore. Voice recordings are not stored — they are processed temporarily for transcription.

5. User Control

You may delete your notes or log out anytime. Account deletion is available upon request.
